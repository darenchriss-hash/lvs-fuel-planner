Welcome! This is a quick guide to the LVS Fuel Planner. It finds the cheapest fuel stops on our Relay network, Pilot, Flying J and One9, at our company discount.
Open the site from the link. It works on a computer and on a phone, and you don't need to log in.
On the left is the trip form. On the right, the fuel plan will appear. Just fill in the form from top to bottom.
Step one: pick the truck. Click Truck number and type it in. You can also type the VIN.
The site pulls this truck from Five ELD automatically: where the truck is right now, how much fuel is in the tank, and when the ELD last updated.
If the ELD data is older than six hours, the site warns you, so call the driver. This button refreshes the ELD data.
Step two: the start city is already filled in from the truck's location. You can change it to any city in the U.S.
Step three: type the delivery city, and pick it from the list. For example, Dallas, Texas.
Is there a next load expected after this delivery? Type it in C, for example Houston, Texas. For more stops, press Add stop. Fuel is planned for the whole trip, so the driver doesn't fuel twice.
Step four: check the truck numbers. The tank size and the fuel level fill in by themselves. MPG is six point five, and thirty gallons is the safety reserve. Only change a number if you know better.
Max off route is how far a station can be from the route. Five miles is good. Departure time is optional.
Step five: press Plan fuel stops.
Here is the plan: the total fuel cost, and how much we save compared to a random Pilot, and to the pump price.
This line shows where each stop is on the route.
The map shows the route, the start, the delivery, and the fuel stops.
For each stop you get the station number, the full address for the driver, our price, how many gallons to buy, and how much fuel will be in the tank.
Step six: send the plan to the driver. Copy the message, or send it by WhatsApp, Telegram, or text, in one click.
Below is the trip guide: when and where to fuel, and the delivery, plus safety tips for the driver.
One tip: if a truck is not in Five ELD, like our TAU trucks on ZigZag, the site tells you. Just type the start city and the fuel level yourself.
And this chart shows the tank size we use for each truck, estimated from our Relay fill-ups. If a size is wrong, just change the tank field.
That's it: truck, delivery, plan fuel stops, and send it to the driver. If you have questions, message Alan.
